# 12 — No rate limiting on scaffolded login/register

**CVSS:** 5.3 (Medium) — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:L/A:N`

**Where:**
- `internal/cli/generator/templates/new/internal/web/server.go.tmpl:129-138` — `/login`, `/register`, and per-field `/login/validate/:field` POSTs registered without `hamrmw.RateLimit(...)`.

## What's wrong

The scaffold ships login, registration, and the HTMX per-field validation endpoints with no throttle. The framework has a rate-limit middleware (`pkg/middleware/ratelimit.go`) and the scaffold imports it elsewhere — it's just never applied to these endpoints.

Compounded by Argon2id's per-attempt cost (~64ms wall clock at the default params) being your only brake, while the per-field validation endpoint will quickly confirm "this email exists" without password verification at all.

## Exploit

- Credential stuffing across a list of leaked email/password pairs — limited only by Argon2 cost per attempt.
- Email enumeration via `/login/validate/email` — no password check, fast turnaround, tells you whether an email is registered.
- Same for `/register/validate/email` (returns "already registered" without further checks).

## Fix

The scaffold should apply a stricter rate limit to the auth routes by default. Suggested:
- `/login` and `/register` POST: 5 attempts per IP per 15 minutes.
- `/login/validate/:field` and `/register/validate/:field` POST: 30 attempts per IP per minute, keyed by route+IP.
- Document that the keying needs `IPExtractor` configured (see finding 02).
